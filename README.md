# ParkFinder
ParkFinder is a responsive web app designed to run on mobile phones, tablets and desktop.
The app will automatically find, categorize and map all parking options near you, world-wide.
Play around with the search radius, filter for free parking and find detailed information about the parking option of your choice!
Once you are ready, click the navigate button and be amazed while Google Maps navigates you to your final destination!

## Key features
- Finds lots of parking options near you!
- World wide data coverage by using the OpenStreetMap Overpass API.
- Custom parking space filtering and categorisation logic.
- Select a result and start Google Maps navigation with one click!

### No location data? - No problem!
You can also use ParkFinder without granting access to your location.
Just select the area you want to search by right-clicking the map!


## What purpose has this app?
Over the years, services like Google Maps have become very good at navigating to any fixed destination you wish for.
Say you want to drive to a city center restaurant. Common navigation services will lead you right there flawlessly. But what happens after you arrived at the restaurant's doorstep? Will there be enough space for parking? And where do you go if you can't park in front of the restaurant?
ParkFinder aims to solve this problem.

Drive wherever you want to go without worries and let ParkFinder do its magic on arrival. Maybe free parking just one block away!
